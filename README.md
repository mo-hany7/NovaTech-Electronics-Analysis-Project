# Customer Service Quality Analytics — Excel Dashboard

An interactive, multi-page Excel dashboard analyzing customer service quality from **January to June 2026**, built on data from Support Tickets, NPS Surveys, and Customer Accounts.

The project covers customer satisfaction (CSAT), loyalty (NPS), customer effort (CES), and churn trends across agents, regions, and customer segments — combining interactive dashboards with a written insights summary that turns the numbers into actual findings.

---

## 🏠 Dashboard Structure

| Page | Description |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Navigation hub with key metrics at a glance and a report overview |
| **CSAT Dashboard** | Satisfaction breakdown by ticket, agent, region, issue category, and channel |
| **NPS Dashboard** | Loyalty & recommendation score, broken down by region, quarter, customer status, and segment |
| **Quality Trend Dashboard** | Monthly trends for CSAT, NPS, CES, FCR, churn rate, and CLV — filterable by agent and month |
| **Insights Summary** | Written analysis connecting findings across all three dashboards |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Overall satisfaction sits at a mid-range **51.5%**, with **North America** scoring highest (59%) and **Asia Pacific** lowest (47%)
- **Billing Issues** and **Order Delays** are the top drivers of customer friction
- NPS is positive but relatively weak (**23.45%**), with a concerning dip between March and May
- A notable **contradiction** exists between CSAT and NPS in North America — high satisfaction doesn't always translate into loyalty or advocacy
- The NPS decline (Mar–May) aligns with a rise in Billing/Order Delay issues, suggesting a possible shared root cause
- Clear performance gap between top agents (Priya Nair, Tom Becker) and lower performers (Maria Santos, Nadia Petrov)
- **Live Chat** is the most satisfying channel; **Social Media** is the weakest
